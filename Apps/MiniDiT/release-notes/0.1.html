MiniDiT 0.1

第一个公开版本。 / First public release.

  • 通过 Wi-Fi、USB 或读卡器从相机导入照片和视频，按规则整理到项目，校验并备份到多个位置。
    Import photos and videos from cameras over Wi-Fi, USB or a card reader, organize them into projects, verify them and back them up to several destinations.
  • 导入后交给 Capture One、Lightroom Classic、DaVinci Resolve、Final Cut Pro、Premiere Pro 或剪映专业版。
    Hand media to Capture One, Lightroom Classic, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro or Jianying Pro after import.
  • 索尼相机联机拍摄：实时取景、曝光辅助、改参数、拍完自动导入。
    Tethered shooting for Sony cameras: live view, exposure aids, camera settings and automatic import.
  • 中文和英文界面，跟随系统语言；内置自动更新。
    Chinese and English interface following the system language; built-in automatic updates.
